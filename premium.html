Recipes from your harvest

Each recipe uses crops available in the HYDRONEXIS Marketplace.

Fresh garden salad

Salad · 10 minutes · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 head of romaine or butterhead lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, cut in wedges
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon chopped chives
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil and the juice of 1 lime

Steps

  1. Rinse the leaves in cold water and dry them well.
  2. Tear the lettuce into a bowl and add tomato and radish.
  3. Whisk oil, lime juice and a pinch of salt, pour over and top with chives.

Spinach and pepper sauté

Side dish · 15 minutes · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of fresh spinach
  • 1 red and 1 yellow bell pepper, in strips
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and cook the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the peppers and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the spinach, stir until it wilts and season with salt.

Tomato and chive salsa

Snack · 10 minutes · 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped chives
  • Juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Combine tomato, pepper and chives in a bowl.
  2. Add lime juice and salt, mix and rest for 10 minutes before serving.
